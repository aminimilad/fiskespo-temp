Gry2026

Mornings come back. A concept identity and website for a Stockholm light-therapy clinic that helps people through the dark months, one morning at a time.

Client
Gry Ljusklinik (concept)
Role
Art Direction, Identity, UX & Web Design
Type
Self-initiated, 2026
Images
All drawn in code, no photos

(The problem)

Clinics for winter depression and sleep usually look like hospitals: blue logos, stock photos of smiling people and long lists of symptoms. For someone who can barely get out of bed in December, that is one more grey room.

(The idea)

Gry is Swedish for dawn. The whole identity is one red sun that rises slowly: blurred and sprayed at first, sharper every week, until it is a clean half circle. It works like the treatment does. Nothing happens overnight, but each morning is a little clearer than the last.

(The solution)

A calm site in warm paper, one red and one grotesk. The front page is a single sun on the horizon with a few scattered words. The programme page stacks the four weeks as four suns going from haze to sharp. Booking is a calendar of sunrises: every day shows when the sun comes up, and you book a morning, not an appointment. A daylight chart of Stockholm explains the season in one picture. Every sun, grain and chart is drawn in code, and every page was designed for the phone too.
