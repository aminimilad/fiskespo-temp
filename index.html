(Info)

My name is Milad Amini. I'm a designer who codes, based in Malmö, working across art direction, UX and native iOS, so ideas never get lost between the moodboard and the App Store.

Day to day I design and ship apps that people rely on while they're on the move, next to identities, websites and stores with a strong visual voice. I take products from the first sketch and user interview to pixel-perfect SwiftUI and motion built with GSAP.

I believe in clarity first and craft always: Swiss structure, bold type, honest colour and animation that has a reason to move. Whether it's a brand, a product or a single screen, I care about the details nobody notices until they're missing.
