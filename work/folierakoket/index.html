Foliera Köket2026

A kitchen you already own, made to feel new. Brand, website and an online kitchen configurator for a Malmö studio that wraps kitchens in film.

Client
Foliera Köket, Malmö
Role
Art Direction, UX, Design & Development
Platform
WordPress, GSAP, Lenis

(The problem)

Most people think a new-looking kitchen means a new kitchen: demolition, weeks without a stove and a six-figure bill. Kitchen wrapping solves that for a fraction of the cost, but online it looked like cheap stickers, and nobody could picture the result before paying for it.

(The idea)

Sell the feeling, not the film. Present wrapping as interior design, calm and editorial like a magazine spread, and let people design their own kitchen before anyone comes to measure.

(The solution)

A trilingual site (Swedish, English, Danish) with large serif caps over real kitchens and soft scroll motion. At its heart is “Bygg ditt kök”: pick doors, countertop and handles from over 1 000 finishes, see the kitchen change live and send it as a quote request that becomes a PDF quote with the ROT deduction worked out. A separate page speaks to hotels and property owners.
